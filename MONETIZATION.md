## Layer 1: approved affiliate links

DigitalOcean's [official affiliate page](https://www.digitalocean.com/affiliates) currently directs applicants to CJ. Apply using the actual published site, then wait for acceptance and obtain the approved tracking link. The public page describes recurring commissions, but no rate is embedded in this site's data or earnings projections. The applicable account agreement and programme dashboard govern actual payment.

The [official affiliate agreement](https://www.digitalocean.com/legal/affiliate-program-agreement) requires disclosure and accurate participating domains, and prohibits competing brand-keyword ads, cookie stuffing, self-referrals and misleading links. Do not activate a tracking URL before acceptance. After approval, set the provider's `affiliate_url` in `.ilang/site.ilang`; rebuild. The generated deal page displays a commission disclosure and `rel="sponsored noopener"`. Confirm the final destination and programme-specific coupon rules before publishing.

Hetzner's [official Cloud FAQ](https://docs.hetzner.com/cloud/general/faq/) states its former referral programme was discontinued. Do not reuse old referral codes or invent a commission programme. Keep direct links unless a current written programme is independently confirmed.

OVHcloud US has an [official partner programme](https://us.ovhcloud.com/partner-program/), but that is not evidence of approval for this site or a public coupon-affiliate arrangement. Keep direct links; applicability and commercial terms remain unknown.
