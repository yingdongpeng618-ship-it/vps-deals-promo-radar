# VPS Cost Lens product record
Production magazine rollout approved by the owner. Previous production records are preserved in the rollout evidence directory.
## Platform
Public static web, desktop and mobile, keyboard and touch. Production: https://vpscostlens.com/.
## Stack
Existing Python standard-library builder, GitHub and Cloudflare Pages. Preview styles and local enhancement only; no framework replacement or external font request.
## Users
Inferred from the site and user discussion: visitors checking VPS cost and terms before visiting an official provider.
## Product Purpose
Find a published price and understand its billing context without losing the official evidence.
## Positioning
VPS Cost Lens is an independent price index. No fabricated discounts, performance ranking, customer evidence or awards.
## Operating Context
Search-led reading and price inspection on mobile/desktop. This is the owner-approved production website.
## Capabilities and Constraints
All generated pages share the approved magazine design. Home uses seven actual DigitalOcean observations from the current repository data. Three separate provider pricing guides already exist, with different billing models; their prices are not mixed into a single false-equivalence table. Hostinger's original amounts, formulas, timestamps and source quotations remain. The deterministic builder applies the theme after rendering while retaining canonical URLs, structured data and official evidence. Normal production indexing remains enabled.
## Brand Commitments
English visitor copy; white canvas, black typographic masthead, upright Georgia display, Segoe UI data/body, cobalt blue functional emphasis; no forest-green palette. No glow, decorative grid, gradients, glass, fake chart or invented status indicator.
## Evidence on Hand
Current official-source data/offers.json; existing audited provider articles; local browser tests, screenshots and independent preview and rollout reviews, preserved in the evidence directories. No independent award judging or field performance measurement.
## Product Principles
1. Price and billing scope precede ornament.
2. Preserve exact source evidence.
3. Keep the reading path predictable.
4. Progressive enhancement leaves all price observations readable without JavaScript.
5. Desktop and phone layouts share the same facts.
## Accessibility and Inclusion
Readable contrast, visible focus, native inputs, 44px control/nav targets, keyboard-scrollable data regions and reduced motion. Source links are inline reading links; repeated functional action labels and units are intentionally retained for context (F53 semantic exception). Tables may scroll within their region; the page must not overflow. Desktop contents is open, mobile contents collapses through native details.

## Production rollout
Owner approved the magazine style for the full site. All generated pages now share the masthead, palette, navigation and typography. Existing pricing content, evidence, schemas and canonical URLs remain. Preview robots restrictions and preview-only labels are removed.
